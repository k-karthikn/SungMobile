# Online-only update: manual POCO check

Install `Sung-0.8.1-online-debug.apk` over the app already on your phone. Do not uninstall or clear data.

1. Check that Likes, playlists, saved sessions, lyrics settings and the paused queue still appear.
2. Search and play a YouTube song; check background/lock-screen controls, the mini player, queue and lyrics.
3. Confirm Library and Now Playing have no Device music or Downloads actions, and the app does not ask for audio-file or notification access.
4. If you installed code 12, confirm the update opens normally. Existing completed files may still be in the phone's shared Music folder, but the app will not manage or play them offline.

Please report any data loss, crash, unexpected storage permission prompt or online playback regression. No USB or assistant-run installation is required.
