# Stage 6 — playback convenience

Implemented 18 October 2026 as `0.7.0-stage6`, code 10, with a sleep-timer countdown display patch in `0.7.1-stage6`, code 11. The owner confirmed the patch works on the POCO and reported all Stage 6 functions done. **Stage 6 is accepted.** The signed debug APK and phone checklist are in `Stages/Stage6_Results/` (files removed 30 October 2026).

## Delivered behavior

- Now Playing has speed control from 0.5× to 2.0× with a one-tap 1.0× reset and a persistent Preserve pitch switch. The service applies the setting to its one Media3 player, and session state reports the applied choice. Seeking and lyric timing continue to use the media position.
- A service-owned sleep timer supports 15/30/60-minute presets, 1–240-minute custom duration, end of the current track and end of queue. Replacing or canceling a timer resets any fade. Duration timers use a monotonic elapsed-time deadline, work independently of screen navigation and optionally fade the player's volume over the final 10 seconds without changing system volume. End-of-track pauses before automatic next; manual Next cancels it. End-of-queue waits for natural completion; queue edits cancel it and repeat prevents it from firing. Stop cancels the timer. A cold process restore deliberately cancels the timer and restores playback paused, so an expired timer can never restart audio.
- After at least 30 seconds of actual playback on a recording of 20 minutes or more, the app stores a bounded per-video resume point. Now Playing offers an explicit Resume or Start over choice when the saved point differs from the current position. The app never silently seeks to it. Natural completion and Start over clear the point; Private mode suppresses new points. Points are kept for at most 90 days and 100 videos.
- Named saved sessions capture the ordered queue with duplicate occurrence IDs, current entry/position, repeat/shuffle, speed and pitch setting. Save, preview, rename, delete and Restore paused are reachable from Now Playing and from Library even with no active track. Restore waits for the service result, replaces the queue only after the user chooses it, and never autoplays. There are at most 20 sessions and 500 entries per session; an oversized queue is reported rather than truncated.
- Sessions and resume points use bounded, atomic app-private files separate from Room's active queue/library and DataStore's preferences. No Room schema change was needed. Neither signed audio URLs nor provider continuation tokens are saved. The app ID and debug signing certificate match accepted code 9, allowing an in-place update without clearing Likes, playlists, lyrics or queue data.

## Verification and remaining device checks

The one-minute timer did pause playback at the right time on the owner's phone, but the label stayed at “Pauses in 1 min” because it rounded all positive values below one minute up to one minute. The patch formats the service's already-updating remaining time as `m:ss`, including seconds. It does not change when the timer fires. The owner subsequently confirmed the corrected build works.
